
Exploratory Data Analysis Report - Netflix Dataset

Dataset Overview:
Netflix titles dataset containing movies and TV shows.

Findings:
1. Most content was released after 2010.
2. Movies are more common than TV Shows.
3. United States has the highest number of titles.
4. TV-MA is the most common rating.
5. Missing values exist in director, cast and country columns.

Conclusion:
Netflix content increased rapidly in recent years and movies dominate the platform.
